Built for the data hall

Not just a warehouse lift.

IXOLIFT DataLift models are purpose-built around the practical challenges of handling heavy, valuable equipment between racks and through constrained data centre spaces.

01

Controlled positioning

Designed to help operators position servers accurately at rack height rather than relying on manual lifting.

02

Narrow-aisle mobility

Compact footprints and controlled wheel systems support movement through data halls, doorways and cabinet rows.

03

Oil-free lifting

Electric models use maintenance-free gear systems, avoiding hydraulic oil in sensitive IT environments.

04

Single-operator use

The range is designed to make routine transport, lifting and rack installation manageable by one trained operator.

05

Rack-height reach

Models reach up to 2.4 m, supporting installations across high rack positions without improvised lifting methods.

06

Protect valuable hardware

Controlled lifting helps reduce the risk of injury and accidental damage when handling expensive IT equipment.
