Key features

Built around controlled server handling.

  • Handles centre loads up to 500 kg at heights up to 1.5 m.
  • Lifts 250 kg loads to the full 2.4 m maximum height.
  • 175 mm side-shift platform supports left or right loading.
  • Remote control enables precise positioning from either side.
  • Oil-free drive system suits sensitive data centre environments.
  • Designed and manufactured in Finland.
